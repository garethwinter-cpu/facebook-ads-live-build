23 · Meta personal attributes rule · check Meta’s current Advertising Standards

Loneliness and finances are personal attributes: implying the viewer has them gets the ad rejected. Fake scarcity isn't a Facebook policy line here so much as a trust killer and, in the UK, a consumer-law problem. The fix is always the same: aim the joke at the cat or the situation.

33 · After Hormozi’s value equation · Prize draws: buy-to-enter usually needs a free entry route · Fictional partners

Why a value stack beats a competition: a draw rewards one winner and everyone else feels they lost; a bundle rewards every buyer, so it converts better and refunds less. In the UK, "buy to enter" draws generally need a free entry route or they count as a lottery, so check the rules before running one. The real prize is the list swap: three brands with the same customer, each emailing its list about the joint offer, so each partner meets two new audiences at the cost of a few samples. Ask the room: who already sells to your customer, but doesn't compete with you?
