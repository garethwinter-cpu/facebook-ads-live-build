# Facebook Ads Live Build

Gareth Winter's Facebook Ads Live Build slides, plus four Claude skills.

**Open the slides:** https://garethwinter-cpu.github.io/facebook-ads-live-build/

## Apply the slides to your business

Paste this into Claude:

> Read this deck: https://garethwinter-cpu.github.io/facebook-ads-live-build/deck.md
> Then take me through it slide by slide and apply each slide to my business: [what you sell, who buys it, your website]. Ask me one question at a time when you need information only I have. At the end, give me my brand kit, three insights, nine Facebook ad drafts (three angles x three variations) and a 15-second Reels script for the strongest one.

## What's here

- `index.html`: the slides. Scroll them all, or press **Present** (arrow keys, N for notes, F for full screen).
- `deck.md`: the same slides and speaker notes as plain text, the easiest version for an AI to read.
- `assets/`: slide images.
- `skills/`: four Claude skills for the full guided build.

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| `facebook-ads-brand-kit` | Purpose, values, position, audience, tone of voice, visual identity, proof and offer |
| `facebook-ads-insight` | Customer tensions, human needs (Life-Force 8), the 10 beliefs |
| `facebook-ads-static` | 20 hooks, feed ads, carousels, lead forms, follow-up email, Ads Manager test plan, reading results |
| `facebook-ads-video` | 15-second Reels scripts, funny versions, AI video shot prompts, hook tests |

## Install the skills in Claude

1. Download the four `.zip` files from `skills/`.
2. In Claude, open **Settings**, then **Capabilities**, and upload each zip under **Skills**.
3. Start a new chat and type: **start my brand kit**.

## Notes

Facebook's placements, specs and ad policies change. Check Meta's Business Help Centre and Advertising Standards before you launch. Frameworks are credited to their authors on the slides and inside each skill.
